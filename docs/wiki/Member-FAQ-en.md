# Member FAQ

Answers for **community members** who want to submit a suggestion. For bot operators see [[FAQ]], [[Быстрый-старт]], [[Команды]].

## General

**Do I need to register?**  
No. You only need a Telegram or Discord account and access to the bot / community server.

**Does it cost anything?**  
Free for members. The community admin runs the bot on their own infrastructure.

**What language is the bot UI?**  
Buttons and bot messages are in **Russian**. Admin docs are partly in English ([[Home-en]]).

## Submitting

**Why does the Telegram bot not reply?**  
Make sure you write in **DMs**, not a group. Start with `/start`. If it still does not respond, the bot may be offline — ask moderators or visit [[Discord-сервер]].

**Can I send a long text?**  
No — limit is **400 characters**. Shorten the idea or split it into parts.

**Can I submit anonymously?**  
Yes. Choose anonymous before submitting — the published post shows «Аноним» without your nickname.

**My draft disappeared after a restart**  
Telegram drafts are stored in bot memory (`MemoryStorage`). If the admin restarted the process, start over with `/start`. Submissions **already in moderation** are kept in the database.

## Moderation and publishing

**How long until a decision?**  
Depends on your community moderators. The bot has no fixed SLA.

**Where does an approved post go?**  
Usually the community Telegram channel and/or Discord `#предложка` feed. Exact targets are set by the admin.

**Why was my submission rejected?**  
The moderator may send a reason in DMs. If not, ask moderators directly.

**Can I edit after submitting?**  
Before a decision — reply to the moderator or send a new submission. After approval, only a moderator can change the text (if allowed).

## Telegram and Discord together

**Do I need both Telegram and Discord?**  
No. Use whichever platform you prefer — if the admin connected both, moderation is shared.

**Will a Discord submission appear in Telegram?**  
Only if the admin enabled publishing on both platforms. Otherwise it may publish on one side only.

## Problems and support

**Something is broken — who do I contact?**  
1. Your community moderators.  
2. [[Discord-сервер]].  
3. [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/noki4angel37/suggest-bridge/issues) for bot bugs (no personal data or tokens).

**How do I report a moderator?**  
That is a community matter — contact channel / server owners, not the bot Issues tracker.
