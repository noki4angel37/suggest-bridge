# How to submit a suggestion

Suggest Bridge accepts suggestions from **Telegram** (DM the bot) and **Discord** (slash command or dedicated channel). Both paths feed the same moderation queue.

## Telegram

1. Open the bot in **direct messages** (not in a group).
2. Tap **Start** or send `/start`.
3. Write your text — up to **400 characters**. You can attach a photo, video, sticker, or link.
4. The bot shows a **draft** with buttons to:
   - edit the text;
   - choose **anonymous** or **with name**;
   - **submit for moderation**;
   - cancel.
5. After submission, moderators receive your request. You may get a DM update if a moderator replies.

> Telegram drafts live in bot memory. If the bot was restarted by an admin, start again with `/start`. Submissions already sent to moderation are **not** lost.

## Discord

Two ways — depends on your server setup:

### `/suggest` command

1. On a server with the bot, run `/suggest`.
2. Enter text (up to 400 characters).
3. Choose anonymity and confirm.

### Suggest channel

1. Find the channel for suggestions (often named like `#посты-предложения`).
2. Post your message with text and attachments if needed.
3. The channel message may be hidden immediately — the bot sends a **draft in your DMs** for confirmation.

## Anonymity

| Mode | How it appears when published |
|------|-------------------------------|
| **With name** | Caption includes your nickname |
| **Anonymous** | Caption shows «Аноним» |

Moderators see which mode you chose before publication.

## Limits

- Text — **400 characters max** (main submission body).
- **Anti-flood** — too many submissions in a short time may be temporarily blocked.
- Blocked users cannot submit until an admin removes the block.

## What happens next

| Status | What it means |
|--------|----------------|
| Pending moderation | Moderators see a card with approve / reject buttons |
| Approved | Post appears in the Telegram channel and/or Discord `#предложка` |
| Scheduled | Publication is planned for a specific time |
| Rejected | Moderator may send a reason in your DMs |
